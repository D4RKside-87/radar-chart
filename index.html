Notion API Configuration

1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations and create an integration
2. Copy the Internal Integration Token and paste it above
3. Open your Notion database, click "Share", and invite your integration
4. Copy the database ID from the URL (the 32-char string after the last /)
5. Click "Connect to Notion" above

Property names are auto-fetched from your database. Only change the fields above if your Notion properties are named something other than "Name" and "Rate".
